# Multi-panel mode

Use when requested, or when separately developed framing sets could materially change the next decision within the effort ceiling. Explain the added cost. Do not multiply panels for routine parallel work.

## Composition and allocation

A provisional two-panel setup has eleven logical participants: central coordinator; shared evidence researcher and practice/purpose investigator; two separate facilitators; three drawn contributors per panel. More panels require an explicit bounded composition, not automatic expansion. Counts are design defaults, not optima. Follow the user's composition, freshness and resource limits.

Before any draw, record panel count, fixed/shared duties, each panel's coverage, allocation rule, common packet, exchange and comparison limits, and coordinator perspective. For the default two panels, one facilitator draws six without replacement; allocate zero-based indices 0/2/4 to A and 1/3/5 to B. The other facilitator verifies fingerprint, membership, distinctness and allocation. Retain one receipt; no convenient redraw. If the pool cannot support the declared distinct allocation, report the limitation rather than silently changing it.

## Independent development

Give panels the same original question, constraints and essential evidence. Shared investigators may prepare one packet; contributors can challenge it or request targeted sources. Disclose source concentration and distinct source assignments. Do not call shared retrieval independent confirmation.

Each facilitator administers its own initial commitments, one starting selective exchange, synthesis and reporting check. Neither panel receives the other's proposals or synthesis before both syntheses commit. Honor fresh-participant requests; schedule logical roles in capacity-respecting batches without claiming simultaneous execution or technical isolation.

## Central comparison and joint closure

After both commit, the coordinator compares mechanisms, dependencies, common unsupported premises, useful differences, conditions favoring alternatives and tests. Keep panel sources and consequential objection dispositions. Distinguish coordinator combinations from author revisions or agreement; do not vote away causal or value disagreements.

Each facilitator checks representation of its own panel once. Then deliver one joint checkpoint, with all submitted notes/questions in predeclared panel/slot order and one overall next-action choice. Earlier necessary questions and participant-requested checkpoints still reach the user; joint closure must not delay a required correction. Do not silently defer optional controls. A bounded continuation needs a named uncertainty and limit; no automatic extra panel or appeals round.
